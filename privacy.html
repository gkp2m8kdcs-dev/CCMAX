Privacy Notice

Effective date: September 16, 2026 · App: CCMAX (Cultural Capital Pro) · Version 1.0.0

CCMAX is designed around a single principle: your data never leaves your iPhone. This notice describes exactly what data the app handles, why, and what it does not do. There is no surprise here — there is no server, no account, and no tracking pipeline.

Summary in plain language

  • No login, account, or sign-up of any kind.
  • No analytics, no advertising SDKs, no third-party trackers.
  • No remote servers operated by us — there is no upload, sync, or backup in the cloud.
  • All calculations (scores, projections, exports) run on-device.
  • Photos you attach are stored locally in the app's sandbox.
  • If you delete the app, your data is removed from the device.

1. Data the app stores on your device

The app stores the following information locally in the iPhone sandbox, so it is only accessible to you and the app itself:

  • Profile — name, headline, region, currency, primary goal, monthly income goal, target retirement age, risk tolerance.
  • Cultural assets — title, category, description, acquisition cost, current valuation, monthly recurring revenue, compound growth rate, hours invested, status, cover image, channel-specific data.
  • Revenue records — date, amount, source asset, monetization type, notes.
  • Goals and scenarios — your saved planning targets, custom growth assumptions, horizons, and category focus.
  • Attached images — cover photos you choose from the camera or photo library, stored inside the app sandbox.

2. Permissions the app requests

  • Camera — used only when you tap to capture a new cover image for an asset. The app does not record video in the background.
  • Photo Library (read) — used only when you pick an existing image to attach as a cover. Selected images are copied into the app sandbox.
  • Photo Library (write/add) — used only when you choose to save an exported report image to your photo library.

You can deny any of these permissions and the app will continue to function — only the related feature will be unavailable.

3. Data we do not collect

CCMAX does not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share:

  • Personal identifiers, contact lists, or location data.
  • Usage analytics, telemetry, or crash reports.
  • Advertising identifiers or any device fingerprints.
  • Financial data beyond what you voluntarily enter into the app.

4. Network usage

The app does not maintain a network connection of its own. It does not embed third-party SDKs that would initiate outbound calls. If you tap a link to email support, the standard iOS Mail composer opens — that connection is governed by Apple's Mail and your email provider.

5. Calculations and projections

All Cultural Capital Scores, benchmark comparisons, compound projections, and exports are computed locally on your device using Apple's native frameworks. Projections are analytical estimates, not forecasts, and are not investment advice.

6. Children

The app is intended for users 16 and older. It does not knowingly collect any personal data from anyone, including children.

7. Your choices and control

  • Edit or delete any asset, goal, scenario, or revenue record from inside the app.
  • Reset all data from Settings → Reset All Data. This permanently removes every record and photo from the app sandbox and reloads the sample dataset.
  • Delete the app to remove all associated data from the device.
  • Export your portfolio to CSV or JSON at any time; the file is produced and saved by you.

8. International users

Because no data leaves your device, cross-border data transfer regulations (such as the GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA, or PIPL) have limited direct impact on the app itself. If you contact us by email, your message and the email address you used will be handled according to standard email practices between you and your provider.

9. Changes to this notice

We may update this notice as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected by a new effective date above and, where appropriate, an in-app notice. Because we never collect data remotely, no migration is ever required.

10. Contact

If you have any questions about privacy or this notice, please contact us using the details below. We respond to every message.
