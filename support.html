Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

Everything — assets, revenue records, goals, scenarios, and attached photos — is stored inside the iPhone app sandbox. The app does not upload, sync, or back up to any remote server. See the Privacy Notice for details.

Will my data survive if I reinstall the app?

Not automatically. Because nothing is stored in iCloud or on a server, deleting the app removes its data. Use Settings → Reset All Data → Export to save a CSV or JSON copy before reinstalling.

Can I use CCMAX on iPad or Mac?

Version 1.0.0 is designed exclusively for iPhone on iOS 16 or later. iPad and Mac support are not currently available.

How is the Cultural Capital Score calculated?

The score is a weighted composite of four sub-indices derived from your inputs: diversification across the six asset categories, liquidity based on monetization channels and time-to-revenue, growth based on your compound growth rate assumptions, and ROI based on acquisition cost vs. current valuation plus monthly revenue. Each sub-index is normalized to a 0–100 band before being combined.

Are the projections investment advice?

No. Projections are analytical estimates built from the numbers you enter. They are designed for planning, not for predicting actual returns. Please read the in-app Disclaimer screen and the Terms of Use.

Can I import data from another app?

Version 1.0.0 supports manual entry and CSV/JSON export. Bulk import via CSV is on the roadmap. If you have a specific format you would like supported, please email us.

How do I delete all my data?

Open the app → Settings → Reset All Data → Reset Everything. Confirm the dialog. This permanently removes every asset, record, goal, scenario, and photo, and reloads the sample dataset.

I lost data after switching devices. Can you restore it?

No — because we never receive your data, we cannot restore it. This is the deliberate trade-off for keeping the app fully private. If you change devices, export a CSV or JSON copy first and re-enter the records manually.

The camera or photo library doesn't work.

iOS may have denied permission. Open the iOS Settings app, scroll to CCMAX, and enable Camera and Photos. Return to the app and try again.

How do I request a feature or report a bug?

Send an email to the address above with a short description and, if possible, a screenshot. For bugs, please include your iPhone model and iOS version.

Troubleshooting checklist

  • Make sure your iPhone is running iOS 16 or later (Settings → General → About).
  • Restart the app after granting a new permission.
  • If a chart looks empty, check that at least one asset has a current valuation greater than zero.
  • If exports fail, ensure the destination app (Files, Photos, Mail) has permission to receive the file.
